How to read this

All lists every protocol in the snapshot: the ranked board first, then the rest with their status — already traded, or not ranked and why. Top is the biggest by TVL. Early is tracked for under six months. Watchlist is confirmed airdrops only — the team has publicly committed to a token and an airdrop; a points programme alone does not qualify.

The screen

Ranked protocols record no token and are independent: no bridges, no wrapped or staked products of a token that trades, no exchanges, no chains. The house watchlist always ranks.

An implied value is this board's own claim, worked out from the figures named in Method & sources. It is not a prediction that a token will be issued, or when. “Records no token” is what the data says; it is never proof a protocol has none. Links are shown as text, exactly as the sources record them.
